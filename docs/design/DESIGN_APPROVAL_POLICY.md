# ParkFair design approval policy

The project owner must explicitly approve a substantial visual change before it is integrated into ParkFair. This covers web and mobile screens, navigation, UI components, identity, colour, typography, dashboards, onboarding, splash screens, icons, illustrations, media, 3D, motion, transitions and gestures.

For a major decision, prepare two or three distinct visual concepts under this directory. Attach actual previews or isolated prototypes. For each, describe its interaction model, assets, accessibility, performance costs, strengths and limitations. Record the concepts and scope in the approval register before requesting a decision.

The owner records the concept version or exact preview, target platform, scope and any conditions. Update the register with the decision, date and reference to the owner's approval. A material deviation needs another approval. Silence, earlier brainstorming and the team's preference do not grant approval.

Use synthetic content in prototypes. Keep proposals out of application routes, builds and bundles. Existing presentation remains the baseline while a proposal is pending. Backend, infrastructure and behaviour fixes can proceed independently when they preserve the visual experience.
